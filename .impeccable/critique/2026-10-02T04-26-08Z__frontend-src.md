## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The look is half-grounded in the product. The specific parts are good: the amber refuel hatch shared by both screens, monospace clock times, reasons printed on the tiles, and the "Last 1-hour start is 9:00 PM" caption. The composition is generic. `/` is an appointment picker a dentist could ship unchanged, and `/fleet` is a standard resource Gantt with a duplicate list under it.

The two screens also disagree about what a day is. The manager sees bookings and buffers on a timeline; the dispatcher sees 31 disconnected tiles for one ship and never sees the booking that blocks them. The brief's wry voice ("Welcome, dispatcher") is absent, and navy, amber and condensed caps read as a default ops console.

**Deterministic scan.** The CLI detector found 0 issues in `frontend/src` and `frontend/index.html`. The in-page detector found 3 on `/` and none on `/fleet`:

- **`low-contrast`**: the disabled "Book charter" button is 4.0:1 (#55627a on #c8d2de). The design review independently called it out as looking like a skeleton. Real issue.
- **`repeating-stripes-gradient`**: the refuel hatch on buffer slots. False positive: it is the product's one distinctive mark, and it carries meaning alongside text.
- **`side-tab`**: a 4px bottom border on the "Your charter" heading. False positive: it is a heading rule, not a side tab.

Measurements also showed no horizontal overflow at either width, no undersized touch targets, and 10 distinct font sizes across the app, with the smallest at 11.2px in timeline blocks.

**Visual overlays.** Overlays rendered on `/` in the [Human] browser tab and were confirmed by screenshot. `/fleet` had nothing to draw.

## Overall Impression

The app is careful where correctness matters and generic where it could be helpful. Conflict handling, focus management and honest uncertainty are better than most production apps. The biggest opportunity is to give the dispatcher the same picture of the day the manager already has.

## What's Working

1. **Reasons sit on the control.** "Booked", "Refuel" and "Past" are printed on the disabled tiles with tint, hatch and strike-through, so state never depends on colour alone (`SlotGrid.tsx:38-40`).
2. **Focus management is deliberate.** Esc closes the details panel and returns focus to the row that opened it; the cancel confirmation focuses "Keep booking"; day jumps move focus to the heading naming the new day.
3. **Uncertainty is reported honestly.** A lost response says the booking could not be confirmed and asks you to check, with no auto-retry (`CharterPage.tsx:105-110`). A 409 keeps the typed name and says the slot is no longer available.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] On mobile the booking form is stranded below the grid**
- **Why it matters**: at 390px, picking 9:00 AM leaves "Book charter" about 1,250px further down the page. Nothing in view confirms the pick or points to the next step, and the result notice then appears back above the grid.
- **Fix**: at ≤860px make "Your charter" a sticky bottom bar that appears on selection, showing ship, time, the pilot field and the Book button (`CharterPage.module.css:82-90`).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] The dispatcher gets a wall of tiles, not a picture of the day**
- **Why it matters**: 31 equal tiles have no morning or afternoon structure, :00 and :30 do not align in columns, and comparing ships means changing the Ship select five times. After hours the whole screen is dead tiles.
- **Fix**: add a compact day strip per ship using the fleet timeline's grammar. Group the chosen ship's start times by hour under Morning, Afternoon and Evening, and collapse past hours into one row (`SlotGrid.tsx:16-46`, `SlotGrid.module.css:2-9`).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] "Say why" is broken in three places**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md promises that a missing Cancel button always comes with its reason in visible text. Each of these breaks that promise or leaves a state unexplained:
  - A started booking shows "Started or finished" and simply has no Cancel button (`BookingDetails.tsx:73-88`).
  - A "Refuel" tile before a booking does not say whose refuel it is.
  - A cancelled booking does not show when it was cancelled, though the API returns `cancelledAt`.
- **Fix**: add "Already departed, so it can't be cancelled." in the actions area; relabel the pre-booking buffer tile "Refuel gap"; show the cancellation time in CT.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] The cancel dialog's hierarchy is ambiguous**
- **Why it matters**: "Cancel booking" and "Close" are the same outlined button side by side, and "Cancel booking" takes initial focus. This is the dismiss-or-destroy confusion at the one destructive moment.
- **Fix**: give "Cancel booking" a danger-outline style, put initial focus on "Close", hide "Close" during the confirm step, and name the booking in the question: "Cancel Ryan's 1:00–5:00 PM charter on USS Wanderer?" (`BookingDetails.tsx:87-123`).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] The fleet view repeats itself on desktop and loses the refuel rule on mobile**
- **Why it matters**:
  - Desktop shows every booking twice, doubling tab stops and screen-reader announcements.
  - Mobile hides the timeline, so the refuel buffer disappears entirely.
  - With "Show cancelled" on, a cancelled row under an active one reads as a double booking.
  - The date is not in the URL, so a reload returns to today.
- **Fix**: put the date in the query string, strike through and mute cancelled rows, add a "+30 min refuel, free from 11:00 AM" line to each mobile row, and make the desktop list a compact table (`FleetDashboard.tsx:140-172`).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user)**: after picking a slot by keyboard, Tab walks every remaining open tile before reaching Pilot name; it was the 17th of 18 stops on an open day. There is no deep link to a ship or date, and Duration is a 16-item select.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)**: each booking is announced twice on `/fleet`. The refuel strip is a `span` with only a `title`, so the buffer does not exist for a screen reader (`FleetTimeline.tsx:53-57`). Disabled tiles are skipped by Tab, so their reasons are only reachable in browse mode. Focus rings, dialog focus and labels all pass.

**Casey (mobile)**: the Book button is never in the thumb zone at the moment of decision. The fleet controls wrap so "Previous day" and "Next day" land on different rows. List rows have no affordance showing they open details, and the selection is lost on reload.

## Minor Observations

- "Enter a pilot name to book." sits below the button it explains.
- The "Your charter" summary omits duration.
- Timeline blocks under about 2 hours drop their times, and some pilot names clip.
- The amber refuel hatch is visually louder than the bookings it follows.
- Axis labels are every 2 hours while gridlines are hourly.
- "CT" is printed on all 15 list rows of a busy day.
- After a successful booking there is no link to see it on the fleet view.
- All headings are condensed uppercase, so the five ship names lose their character.

## Questions to Consider

- Why does the manager get the timeline when the dispatcher is the one who has to find a gap?
- Should the dispatcher pick a time window first and be shown which of the five ships can fly it?
- If the timeline can open a booking, why can't an empty stretch of it create one?
- Where is "now"? The product is built on the server's clock and neither screen shows it.
